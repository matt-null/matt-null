### What I do

I build things that have to work on day one, for real businesses and real hardware:

- 🪟 **Windows apps and utilities** — desktop software in C# and WinUI 3
- 🌐 **Web apps and portals** — customer-facing tools, dashboards, internal systems
- 🏪 **Business systems** — point of sale, loyalty, inventory, the plumbing a shop runs on
- 🏠 **Smart home and security** — connected setups, automation, camera and alarm installs
- 🎮 **Game servers** — setup, management and custom plugins

I also manage a retail business, so most of what I build starts as something I needed myself.
